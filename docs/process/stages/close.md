# CLOSE — close obligations, preserve useful knowledge

The driver accounts for the accepted goal revision, every required residual,
known defect and external promise. Every invariant needs executed end-to-end
proof; a green slice, source grep, warning or backlog entry is insufficient.

1. An independent reviewer verifies the goal's end-to-end evidence (it may be
   the final slice reviewer when the same tree and complete goal were reviewed).
   Reuse that result only for unchanged obligations and evidence. Unproven work
   stays open; a technical limit is not permission to drop a requirement.
2. Resolve required open questions and residuals. A changed destination needs
   the user's recorded amendment (`RDY-6`), not a fictional completed goal.
   Deferred useful work outside the accepted result has an explicit owner and
   trigger; never call a required repair complete by moving it to backlog.
3. Export durable decisions, operating constraints and reproducible evidence
   needed after delivery. Routine observations and failed attempts remain in
   git history; no obligation to mint a carrier or `dropped:` line for each.
4. Remove completed goal/item documents and stale links; keep review/evidence
   artifacts referenced by the delivery. CHANGELOG, `backlog:check`, then merge
   when the reviewed result still matches (`REV-8`).

Done means the accepted result is proven and required residuals are resolved.
Deletion is cleanup, never the definition of success.
